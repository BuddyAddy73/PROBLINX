# Problinx

**A place where problems meet the people willing to actually solve them — and prove it.**

---

## What it is

Most of the internet is full of threads like *"what should I use for X?"* — buried in Reddit comments, forgotten Discord messages, or drowned out in a Product Hunt feed optimized for launch-day hype rather than whether the thing actually works.

Problinx flips that. You post a real problem. Builders pitch real solutions — either something that already exists, or something they'd build from scratch. And before anyone's vote counts, they have to actually go test the thing for themselves. No upvoting based on vibes. No astroturfed five-star reviews from accounts that never touched the product. If you didn't try it, you don't get a say.

It's a small, honest idea: **let the people who need something and the people who can build it find each other, and make sure the feedback in between is real.**

---

## How it works

**1. Post a problem.**
Something you're stuck on, something that doesn't have a good tool yet, something you keep half-solving with a spreadsheet. Say what it is, what you've already tried, and let it go up on the Wishlist.

**2. Builders pitch back.**
Anyone can respond in one of two ways:
- **"I've got something that already exists"** — a real tool, with a link, ready to try right now.
- **"I'd build this"** — a proposed approach, an honest timeline for how long it'd take, and a way to reach them if you're interested.

**3. Nobody votes blind.**
Before a review counts toward a solution's rating, the reviewer has to open the link, spend 60 real seconds actually using it, and only then are they allowed to leave a review and a score. The countdown is visible. You can't skip it by clicking away — the app locks you into the test, on purpose.

**4. The best answer rises, earned, not guessed.**
Ratings build up from people who genuinely used the thing, not from whoever showed up first or has the most followers.

## What you can actually do right now

- **Post problems** to the public Wishlist, with a short writeup of what you've already tried
- **Pitch a solution** — as an existing tool with a live link, or as a proposal with an honest build timeline
- **Test and review** a pitched solution through the guarded 90-second flow, with your rating and written feedback attached to your real account
- **Upvote problems** you want solved (one vote per person, no spamming the button)
- **Sign in with Google**, or just browse anonymously if you're not ready to commit to an account yet
- **Set a tag on your profile** — Builder, Scout, or Surfer — describing how you actually show up here, changeable once every two days so it stays honest rather than something you flip on a whim
- **Search for other users** (`Ctrl/Cmd + K` from anywhere) and see everything they've posted or pitched, in one place
- **Switch between dark and light mode**, because it's 2026 and that shouldn't need explaining

That's the whole loop, working, live, right now. No fake data, no smoke and mirrors — every piece above is something you can actually click and use today.

## Why this is different from what's already out there

**Reddit "what should I use" threads** get you opinions, fast, but they're unstructured, un-searchable six months later, and nobody's checking whether the top comment actually tried the thing they're recommending.

**Product Hunt** is built for launch-day noise — a good tool that launches quietly gets buried, and a mediocre one with a slick teaser video can win the day. It's optimized for attention, not fit.

**AlternativeTo / G2 / Capterra** are review aggregators, but the reviews are self-reported, unverified, and often incentivized — you're trusting a stranger's word with no proof they used the product for more than five minutes.

**Problinx's actual difference is small and specific, on purpose:** the test-before-you-vote mechanic isn't a gimmick, it's the entire point. It's slower than an upvote button, and that's intentional — it filters out the noise that makes every other platform's "top answer" a coin flip.

We're not trying to be everything. We're trying to be the one place where, if something has a good rating, you can actually trust it means something.

## Where it stands today

This is an early, working MVP — not a mockup, not a demo, a real app with a real database behind it. Features are being added deliberately, one at a time, tested before they ship. If something feels rough around the edges, it probably is — we're building this in the open, honestly, rather than pretending it's finished.

More is coming. This is just where it starts.
